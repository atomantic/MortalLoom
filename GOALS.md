# MortalLoom Goals

## Mission

Help the user reach **Longevity Escape Velocity (LEV)** — the point where life expectancy increases faster than time passes, effectively making aging a solvable problem rather than a terminal condition.

## What is Longevity Escape Velocity?

LEV is the theoretical threshold where advances in medical science extend life expectancy by more than one year for every year that passes. Once achieved, each year of life grants more than a year of additional expected lifespan through new treatments, therapies, and interventions.

The concept was popularized by Aubrey de Grey, who estimates LEV could be reached by 2035-2045 for those who maintain good health until then. The key insight: **you don't need to solve aging today — you just need to survive long enough for the science to catch up.**

## How MortalLoom Helps

### 1. Know Your Baseline
- Calculate life expectancy from actuarial tables (SSA data)
- Adjust for genetics via genome analysis and ClinVar pathogenicity
- Track epigenetic (biological) age vs chronological age
- Visualize your life progress and remaining time

### 2. Track What Matters
- Monitor blood biomarkers with reference ranges to catch issues early
- Track body composition trends over time
- Log substance intake (alcohol, nicotine) with clinical risk thresholds
- Integrate Apple Health metrics: steps, heart rate, HRV, sleep, VO2 max

### 3. Optimize Your Trajectory
- Lifestyle questionnaire quantifies how habits affect longevity
- Correlation analysis shows how substances impact biomarkers
- Rolling averages reveal trends before they become problems
- Longevity clock creates urgency and motivation to optimize your health

### 4. Bridge to LEV
- LEV 2045 progress tracker monitors research milestones
- Every year you extend your health buys time for new science
- The app shows exactly how lifestyle changes buy additional years
- Goal: keep your biological age younger than your chronological age

## Privacy as a Feature

Health data is the most sensitive data a person has. MortalLoom is built on the principle that **privacy is not a tradeoff — it's a requirement.**

- No accounts, no logins, no servers
- No data collection, no telemetry, no analytics
- No third-party SDKs, no ad networks, no tracking
- Data lives on-device and in your personal iCloud
- Export anytime in standard formats — your data is always yours

## Target User

People who are serious about extending their healthspan and lifespan, who want comprehensive tracking without surrendering their most personal data to a subscription service.
