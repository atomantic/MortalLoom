# MortalLoom Goals

## Mission

Help you align your time with what matters most to you, extend that time as long as possible, and measure whether you're actually doing the first two.

**Time is the one resource that gates everything else.** Every goal — creative, relational, financial, legacy — eats time to achieve. So MortalLoom does two things together:

1. **Aligns how you spend your time** with the goals you set (North Stars → life pillars → concrete goals).
2. **Extends how much time you have** through deep health and longevity tracking that protects your healthspan and surfaces interventions before problems become terminal.

These two halves reinforce each other. Better alignment means you're not wasting the time you have. Better longevity means you have more time to achieve what you set out to do. Neither half makes sense alone.

## Why time awareness matters

Most productivity apps optimize day-to-day task throughput. MortalLoom optimizes the direction of a whole life. The mortality clock isn't a morbid gimmick — it's a forcing function that makes "someday" concrete. If your North Star is "leave a lasting creative legacy" and you haven't touched creative work in four months, the app notices, and asks you what's blocking you. If your goal timeline extends past your estimated healthy cognitive years, the app flags it and asks you to accelerate or accept.

## How it works

### 1. Name your North Star

Your single biggest life purpose. Broad, lifelong, no end date. Examples:

- Live healthy for as long as possible
- Leave a lasting creative legacy
- Raise a loving, resilient family
- Achieve financial independence and autonomy
- Deepen mastery in my craft

This is the thing everything else serves. North Stars don't have progress bars — they're directions, not destinations.

### 2. Build your life pillars

The 3–6 areas that support your North Star. These are also lifelong — think "build strong physical fitness", "achieve financial independence", "deepen craft mastery". They give the North Star structure without pretending to be tasks.

### 3. Set concrete goals

Dated, progress-trackable goals that feed into a life pillar. These *do* have deadlines, milestones, and % complete. This is where your calendar fills up. Examples: "run a half-marathon by June", "publish first novel by end of year", "save $20,000 for a down payment".

### 4. Measure alignment

MortalLoom rolls up how your active concrete goals are doing and shows an **Alignment Score** under each North Star: how much of your recent effort has moved the needle on what you said mattered. This isn't "progress toward your lifetime purpose" (lifetime purposes don't complete) — it's "how aligned is your recent life with your stated values".

### 5. Reflect when you stall

When the app notices stagnation — a pillar with no recent check-ins, a North Star with no active supporting goals, a calendar with no work blocks pointing at your top priorities — it prompts reflection:

- *"Are you still aligned with your North Star?"*
- *"What's holding you back right now?"*
- *"Which tasks or habits are preventing you from moving toward your North Star?"*
- *"What could you clear from your calendar this week to make room?"*

These prompts appear during onboarding (to ground the first use) and throughout the app as stagnation is detected.

### 6. Watch the runway

Your life expectancy estimate is always visible — not to create anxiety, but to keep the question honest: *is today's tradeoff one you'll be glad you made?*

## Health and longevity: the runway extender

Health tracking is a first-class feature of MortalLoom, not a side module — because **every year you extend your healthspan buys more time for every other goal you care about**. Blood markers, body composition, Apple Health metrics, substance tracking, epigenetic age, and the longevity clock all exist to answer one question: *how much time do you actually have, and how do you get more of it?*

The app's runway estimate — actuarial baseline adjusted for genetics, biomarkers, lifestyle, and Apple Health data — feeds directly into the goals system:

- Goal timelines are checked against remaining healthy cognitive years. If a 20-year goal plan projects past your healthy cognitive horizon, MortalLoom warns you.
- Alignment math weights time explicitly: the less runway you have, the more "this week" matters.
- Longevity-extending interventions (sleep, exercise, diet, sauna, avoiding alcohol/nicotine) show up as concrete leverage points for users whose North Star touches "live healthy for as long as possible".

For health-focused users, MortalLoom also tracks progress toward **Longevity Escape Velocity (LEV)** — the theoretical threshold where medical science extends life expectancy by more than one year per calendar year. The concept was popularized by Aubrey de Grey, who estimates LEV could be reached by 2035–2045 for those who maintain good health until then. The key insight: **you don't need to solve aging today — you just need to survive long enough for the science to catch up.** LEV 2045 is the specific milestone the app currently tracks.

## Health tracking is integrated, not mandatory

The deep health analytics are most relevant to users whose North Star or life pillars touch health. Users whose North Star is creative, relational, financial, spiritual, or legacy-driven get the same first-class treatment of the goals system — they simply engage with the longevity clock as a time-remaining dashboard rather than as a daily optimization target.

**Practical note on current state**: the health tracking module is the most built-out surface area because it's the developer's personal focus. The goals system is newer and needs work. Current priorities re-center the Overview around goals (with the runway clock as a supporting element rather than the centerpiece) and broaden out-of-the-box support for non-health pillars.

## Privacy as a requirement

Goal data and health data are the most sensitive data a person has. MortalLoom is built on the principle that **privacy is not a tradeoff — it's a requirement.**

- No accounts, no logins, no servers
- No data collection, no telemetry, no analytics
- No third-party SDKs, no ad networks, no tracking
- Data lives on-device and in your personal iCloud
- Export anytime in standard formats — your data is always yours

## Target user

People who want to spend their finite time on what matters most to them, and want tools that help without surrendering their most personal data to a subscription service. The current build is strongest for users whose goals touch health and longevity, but the foundation is meant to serve any lifetime purpose.
